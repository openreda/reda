# How other EDA tools present and edit the stackup

Research for REDA issue #320. Read on 2026-11-18. The comparison uses editor manuals, vendor release notes, illustrated training material and fabricators’ specifications. KiCad 9.0 is the comparison version. KiCad 10.0 is identified separately where its impedance workflow differs. Descriptions of screenshot contents come from the web reader’s figure transcriptions, captions or alt text. No editor was run interactively. [KiCad 9 manual](https://docs.kicad.org/9.0/en/pcbnew/pcbnew.html) · [KiCad 10 release announcement](https://www.kicad.org/blog/2026/03/Version-10.0.0-Released/)

## Answer in brief

- **The leading suite** has three distinct drawings. A separate 2D/3D visualizer shows the physical construction and can display realistic relative thicknesses. The **Via Types** tab aligns a drawing of each allowed Via span with the Stackup rows. The **Impedance** tab draws the selected Track geometry in the Properties panel and solves width, gap or impedance. Structural edits normally use the grid. [Stackup manual](https://www.altium.com/documentation/altium-designer/pcb/defining-layer-stack) · [Via span figure](https://files.resources.altium.com/sites/default/files/uberflip_docs/file_723.pdf) · [Impedance figure](https://files.resources.altium.com/sites/default/files/2020-03/Impedance%20Calculation_fin2.pdf)
- **A second commercial suite** puts a spreadsheet and a dockable cross-section side by side. Its drill picture reflects padstacks actually used on the board. The user can reverse the displayed drill direction for the drill legend. A separate Via List Viewer shows the Vias available to a routing constraint set, including their start and end Layers. Impedance estimates appear in the Stackup spreadsheet. [Cross-section editor](https://community.cadence.com/cadence_blogs_8/b/pcb/posts/10-top-reasons-to-move-up-to-allegro-17-2-2016-release) · [Release notes, pp. 36–40](https://www.scribd.com/document/393459436/Release-Notes-pdf) · [Via List Viewer](https://community.cadence.com/cfs-file/__key/telligent-evolution-components-attachments/00-27-01-00-00-96-42-32/cmug.pdf) · [Impedance columns](https://www.flowcad.de/datasheets/24-1-Whats-New.pdf)
- **KiCad 9.0** represents the Stackup as one marked row per physical entry. The illustrated **Physical Stackup** page has no separate cross-section, thickness handles or Via-span overlay. Routing Layer pairs and the transmission-line calculator have their own interfaces. [Illustrated PCB Editor manual](https://docs.kicad.org/9.0/en/pcbnew/pcbnew.pdf) · [Calculator Tools](https://docs.kicad.org/9.0/en/pcb_calculator/pcb_calculator.html)
- **Fusion Electronics** puts Layer properties, a Via-pair list and a cross-section preview in one window. Choosing two Via endpoints updates both the list and the picture. Layer pairs, their materials and the Via definitions can travel together in a preset. The documented impedance readout is on the routed board in PCB Simulation. [Stackup editor](https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/ENU/Fusion-ECAD/files/ECD-SETTINGS-MULTILAYER-TSK.htm) · [Illustrated guide, p. 5](https://damassets.autodesk.net/content/dam/autodesk/www/pdfs/a-comprehensive-guide-to-signal-integrity-simulation-in-pcb-design--what-you-need-to-know.pdf) · [Impedance analysis](https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/ENU/Fusion-ECAD/files/ECD-IMPEDANCE-ANALYSIS-TSK.htm)

## The leading suite

### Editor layout

**Design → Layer Stack Manager** opens a document tab alongside the PCB. In the workshop example, sketch a Properties panel at the left, a broad spreadsheet in the centre and a strip of tabs along the bottom. The rows run from the top face to the bottom face. **Stackup**, **Impedance** and **Via Types** reuse this vertical ordering, changing what appears beside the rows. Properties can also be docked on the right, as in the impedance example. **Tools → Layerstack Visualizer** opens a separate window over the document. Saving the Stackup makes its changes available in the PCB editor. [Editor manual](https://www.altium.com/documentation/altium-designer/pcb/defining-layer-stack) · [Workshop figures](https://files.resources.altium.com/sites/default/files/2020-10/Altium%20Designer%E5%85%A5%E9%96%80%20-%20PCB%E8%A8%AD%E8%A8%88%20%E3%83%AF%E3%83%BC%E3%82%AF%E3%82%B7%E3%83%A7%E3%83%83%E3%83%95%E3%82%9A%E8%B3%87%E6%96%99.pdf) · [Impedance example](https://files.resources.altium.com/sites/default/files/2020-03/Impedance%20Calculation_fin2.pdf)

### Cross-section drawing and direct interaction

The **2D view** is an edge view. In a co-branded rigid-flex workshop screenshot, the rigid region on the left is a stack of horizontal bands. A band low in that stack continues to the right into the thinner flex region. The controls are below the picture. **2D** and **Show full stack** are selected in this example, and **Real layers height** is off. In the vendor’s **3D** workshop figure, the top is a patterned green face. Orange copper is visible between grey insulating entries at the exposed edge, and part of the stack steps back to reveal successive strata. These details come from the figure transcriptions. [2D workshop figure](https://www.mdstech.co.kr/data/mds/old/133.pdf) · [3D workshop figure](https://files.resources.altium.com/sites/default/files/2020-10/Altium%20Designer%E5%85%A5%E9%96%80%20-%20PCB%E8%A8%AD%E8%A8%88%20%E3%83%AF%E3%83%BC%E3%82%AF%E3%82%B7%E3%83%A7%E3%83%83%E3%83%95%E3%82%9A%E8%B3%87%E6%96%99.pdf)

**Real layers height** displays realistic relative thicknesses. Both 2D and 3D support scaled views, including Via structures. The user can also add display gaps, show names, simplify conductor patterns and switch camera projection. Right-drag reorients the model. Clicking the image and copying captures it. The manual gives no calibrated screen scale or gesture for changing material thickness by dragging an edge. [Visualizer controls](https://www.altium.com/documentation/altium-designer/pcb/defining-layer-stack) · [Vendor description of scale and Via rendering](https://files.resources.altium.com/sites/default/files/uberflip_docs/file_870.pdf)

There is a direct structural gesture in the **Board** tab for advanced rigid-flex designs. Shift-click selects entries in an existing substack. Dragging that selection **horizontally** creates and positions another substack. Double-clicking a substack opens it for editing. This operation is documented for the Board tab, separately from the visualizer. [Board tab instructions](https://www.altium.com/documentation/altium-designer/pcb/defining-layer-stack)

### Via spans

On **Via Types**, draw the names and thicknesses on the left. Continue the horizontal bands across the area to their right. Give each Via definition a heading across the top and draw its barrel vertically beneath that heading, ending on the corresponding copper rows. The vendor’s eight-Layer HDI figure shows a through Via running from 1 to 8, a buried Via from 2 to 7, and shorter, tapered microvias near the faces. A mirrored pair can share one heading, with both endpoint pairs written in it. The column layout and barrel shapes are visible in the vendor figure transcription. The Via manual independently explains that the orientation of the conical microvia indicates drilling direction. [HDI span figure](https://files.resources.altium.com/sites/default/files/uberflip_docs/file_723.pdf) · [Via definition manual and figure captions](https://www.altium.com/documentation/altium-designer/pcb/blind-buried-micro-vias)

The columns represent **allowed types**. Select a definition and set **First layer** and **Last layer** in Properties. The editor derives the through, blind or buried classification from the endpoints. The default through span is permanent. The microvia option accepts adjacent Layers or a skip of one Layer. Enabling Stackup symmetry makes a **Mirror** option available. The Via’s hole and Pad diameters are specified separately from its span. [Via definition manual](https://www.altium.com/documentation/altium-designer/pcb/blind-buried-micro-vias)

### Table columns and per-Layer and per-Dielectric data

The workshop shows **Name, Material, Type, Weight, Thickness, Dk** and **Df**. Users can show, hide and reorder columns. Copper can be a signal or plane Layer, with foil weight per unit area, thickness and manufacturing process. A Dielectric can specify core or prepreg, material, thickness, relative permittivity (Dk), dissipation factor (Df) and the test frequency associated with those electrical values. Glass construction and resin content are available. Outer rows can include solder mask and silkscreen, labelled **Overlay**. [Stackup fields](https://www.altium.com/documentation/altium-designer/pcb/defining-layer-stack) · [Illustrated Stackup example](https://files.resources.altium.com/sites/default/files/2020-03/Impedance%20Calculation_fin2.pdf)

### Adding, removing and reordering

Insert above or below the selected row. Adding copper beside existing copper also inserts a Dielectric between them. The grid’s move-up and move-down commands move an entry among entries of the same type. Deleting a Layer that contains board objects prompts for confirmation. [Grid commands](https://www.altium.com/documentation/altium-designer/pcb/defining-layer-stack) · [Illustrated insertion example](https://my.altium.com/altium-designer/evaluation-guide/managing-pcb-layer-stackup)

### Templates and material libraries

**Tools → Presets** supplies fixed starting constructions. Users can save their own Stackup templates locally or in a shared workspace. The material cell opens a picker filtered to the selected entry type. **Tools → Material Library** manages material definitions and can load or save user materials in XML. **Library Compliance** flags a board value that differs from its chosen library record. [Presets, templates and material library](https://www.altium.com/documentation/altium-designer/pcb/defining-layer-stack)

### Symmetry check

Enabling **Stack Symmetry** checks entries equidistant from the central Dielectric and lists mismatches. The user can copy the upper half onto the lower half, copy the lower half upwards, or extend and mirror the whole construction. Later additions and property edits propagate to the partner. [Symmetry check and repair options](https://www.altium.com/documentation/altium-designer/pcb/defining-layer-stack)

### Impedance readout

On **Impedance**, the left side of the grid retains the physical rows. The right side has a checkbox for each eligible signal Layer and columns for its upper and lower references, Track width, gap and calculated impedance. Selecting an enabled row fades the Stackup entries outside that calculation. In the illustrated differential example, the **Properties** panel is on the far right. Beneath the profile type, target and tolerance sits a small **Transmission Line** cross-section showing two conductors in the surrounding Dielectric, with their widths and separation labelled. This is the drawing of the selected routing geometry. [Controlled-impedance manual](https://www.altium.com/documentation/altium-designer/pcb/high-speed-design/interactively-routing-controlled-impedance) · [Annotated calculation example](https://files.resources.altium.com/sites/default/files/2020-03/Impedance%20Calculation_fin2.pdf)

Entering a target solves for width. Entering a width gives the corresponding impedance. For a differential pair, the user can hold width or gap fixed and solve the other. The solver documentation gives a default calculation frequency of **1 GHz**. The material’s Dk/Df test frequency is a separate field. [Solver behaviour](https://www.altium.com/documentation/altium-designer/pcb/high-speed-design/interactively-routing-controlled-impedance) · [Material fields](https://www.altium.com/documentation/altium-designer/pcb/defining-layer-stack)

## The second commercial suite

### Editor layout

The current getting-started guide opens **Tools → Cross-Section**. The classic PCB editor uses **Setup → Cross-section** or the `xsection` command. Sketch a wide spreadsheet on the left and a narrower, dockable drawing on the right. Read both from the top surface downwards. In the six-Layer guide figure the drawing is labelled **Primary**. It shows the top conductor, inner conductors and reference Layers interleaved with Dielectrics, then the bottom conductor and surface. A strip beneath the spreadsheet reports total thickness with and without masks and counts conductors, plane Layers and masks. Multiple constructions have named tabs, with an overview across them. The pane positions, band labels and totals come from the guide figure. [Getting-started guide](https://resources.academic.cadence.com/pcb-design-and-analysis-resources/2025-getting-started-with-orcad-x) · [Six-Layer editor figure, p. 27](https://www.ema-eda.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/orcad-x-constraint-management-guide-part-5.pdf) · [Classic editor and multiple Stackups](https://community.cadence.com/cadence_blogs_8/b/pcb/posts/specifying-layer-info-multilayer-rigid-flex-stackups)

### Cross-section drawing and direct interaction

The drawing uses coloured horizontal bands and vertical drill indicators. It updates when the user changes which entries belong to a construction. One action operates on the drawing itself. In the 17.2 release notes, the user points to a drill symbol to reverse the direction displayed for a blind Via, buried Via or microvia. The documented effect is the ordering of the endpoint Layers in the NC drill legend. Thickness and material edits use spreadsheet fields. A proportional band-height mode or a draggable thickness boundary is not stated in the documentation reviewed. [Dynamic picture and drill-direction control](https://community.cadence.com/cadence_blogs_8/b/pcb/posts/10-top-reasons-to-move-up-to-allegro-17-2-2016-release) · [Release notes, pp. 36–40](https://www.scribd.com/document/393459436/Release-Notes-pdf) · [Updating the drawing from the table](https://community.cadence.com/cadence_blogs_8/b/pcb/posts/why-move-up-to-allegro-17-2-2016-so-how-does-your-design-stack-up-reason-5-of-10)

### Via spans

There are two places to inspect spans, and they answer different questions.

- **Which drills does this board use?** The 17.2 release notes say the cross-section draws drills from padstacks actually used in the board database. A Via allowed by a physical constraint set appears in this drawing once it is used. The illustrated editor shows vertical arrows against the named bands, and the vendor identifies drilling direction as part of the picture. [Release notes, pp. 36–40](https://www.scribd.com/document/393459436/Release-Notes-pdf) · [Editor figure, p. 27](https://www.ema-eda.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/orcad-x-constraint-management-guide-part-5.pdf)
- **Which Vias may be used for routing?** A padstack defines the hole, Pads and Layer span. In the documented **Edit Via List** dialog, the left list contains library or board candidates and the right list contains the chosen Vias. Both lists have **Name, Start** and **End** columns. The figure shows `TOP` and `BOTTOM` for a through Via. **Show Viewer** opens a lower pane with a graphical cross-section of the selected constraint set’s Vias against labelled, coloured Layers. That layout is from the 16.3 manual. The 25.1 release notes still identify separate constraints-panel controls for editing the Via list and opening a Via cross-section. [Padstack and HDI guide, pp. 3–6](https://www.ema-eda.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/designing-hdi-in-allegro-x.pdf) · [Via List Viewer, pp. 39–40](https://community.cadence.com/cfs-file/__key/telligent-evolution-components-attachments/00-27-01-00-00-96-42-32/cmug.pdf) · [25.1 release notes](https://www.flowcad.de/datasheets/OrCADX-AllegroX-Release-25-1-Whats-New.pdf)

### Table columns and per-Layer and per-Dielectric data

The six-Layer example groups columns by purpose. **Objects** contains the name. **Types** includes Layer type and function. **Thickness** has a nominal value and separate positive and negative tolerances. **Physical** includes Layer ID and material. Copper rows distinguish **Conductor** and **Plane**. The figure shows copper weight in a material name such as **1oz Copper**, alongside a numeric thickness. Other documented fields include conductivity, etch factor and a shield setting for a reference Layer. [Editor figure, p. 27](https://www.ema-eda.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/orcad-x-constraint-management-guide-part-5.pdf) · [Field descriptions](https://community.cadence.com/cadence_blogs_8/b/pcb/posts/specifying-layer-info-multilayer-rigid-flex-stackups)

Dielectric entries carry material, thickness, Dk and loss tangent. The HDI guide distinguishes core from prepreg and starting foil from finished copper. The basic editor figure does not establish a dedicated core/prepreg selector, an independently editable foil-weight column or a dielectric test-frequency column. [HDI construction guide, pp. 4–5](https://www.ema-eda.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/designing-hdi-in-allegro-x.pdf) · [Editor fields](https://community.cadence.com/cadence_blogs_8/b/pcb/posts/specifying-layer-info-multilayer-rigid-flex-stackups)

### Adding, removing and reordering

Right-click **Objects**, choose **Add Layers**, specify type, function, material and insertion position, then add the entries. The editor supports pairs or a user-specified number, and the getting-started guide documents removal. For masks, the vendor documents **Edit Mask Layer Order** on the Layer-name menu of its package editor. It moves masks up or down relative to copper and Dielectrics. A general move-up/down or drag command for populated copper Layers in the PCB editor was not verified. [Insertion workflow](https://community.cadence.com/cadence_blogs_8/b/pcb/posts/specifying-layer-info-multilayer-rigid-flex-stackups) · [Pair and count controls](https://community.cadence.com/cadence_blogs_8/b/pcb/posts/10-top-reasons-to-move-up-to-allegro-17-2-2016-release) · [Removal](https://resources.academic.cadence.com/pcb-design-and-analysis-resources/2025-getting-started-with-orcad-x) · [Mask ordering](https://community.cadence.com/cadence_blogs_8/b/pcb/posts/accurate-masking-of-your-substrate-layers)

### Templates and material libraries

A finished construction can be exported as a cross-section technology file. **Import → Cross-Section Technology File** loads a `.tcfx` construction, including Layer structure, names and thicknesses. Material choices come from a separate editable `materials.dat` file. The vendor documents the supplied file under `share/pcb/text` in the installation and describes adding and removing material records. [Technology-file export](https://community.cadence.com/cadence_blogs_8/b/pcb/posts/why-move-up-to-allegro-17-2-2016-so-how-does-your-design-stack-up-reason-5-of-10) · [Technology-file import](https://resources.academic.cadence.com/pcb-design-and-analysis-resources/2025-getting-started-with-orcad-x) · [Materials file](https://community.cadence.com/cadence_blogs_8/b/pcb/posts/specifying-layer-info-multilayer-rigid-flex-stackups)

### Symmetry check

The HDI guide discusses comparing copper coverage on opposite Layer pairs when assessing warpage. Adding entries in pairs is also documented. A Cross-section Editor command that compares the materials and thicknesses of opposite entries, lists mismatches or mirrors a repair is not stated in the reviewed documentation. [HDI guide, §7.3](https://www.ema-eda.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/designing-hdi-in-allegro-x.pdf) · [Paired additions](https://community.cadence.com/cadence_blogs_8/b/pcb/posts/10-top-reasons-to-move-up-to-allegro-17-2-2016-release)

### Impedance readout

The editor displays single-ended and differential impedance for microstrip and stripline. Differential coupling can be edge-coupled or broadside-coupled. In the 24.1 release figure, a named physical constraint set contributes spreadsheet columns for whether routing is allowed on a Layer, its Track width and **Est. Z0**, the estimated characteristic impedance. This places the result on the corresponding Layer row. [Impedance fields](https://community.cadence.com/cadence_blogs_8/b/pcb/posts/specifying-layer-info-multilayer-rigid-flex-stackups) · [24.1 editor figure, p. 92](https://www.flowcad.de/datasheets/24-1-Whats-New.pdf)

## KiCad 9.0

### Editor layout

Open **File → Board Setup → Board Stackup → Physical Stackup**. Sketch a settings tree at left and a table filling the rest of the dialog. Across the top are **Copper layers**, **Impedance controlled** and the Dielectric add/remove buttons. Below the last row are the calculated board thickness, **Adjust Dielectric Thickness** and **Export to Clipboard**. Board-settings import and the dialog buttons sit at the foot. **Board Editor Layers** is a sibling page in the tree. [KiCad 9 PCB Editor manual](https://docs.kicad.org/9.0/en/pcbnew/pcbnew.html) · [Physical Stackup figure](https://docs.kicad.org/9.0/en/pcbnew/pcbnew.pdf)

### Cross-section drawing and direct interaction

The physical order is visible **in the table itself**. The first column has a small visual marker for each entry. The next column identifies it. In the four-Layer example the rows descend from front silkscreen, paste and mask through front copper, alternating Dielectrics and inner copper, then back copper, mask, paste and silkscreen. There is no separate drawing beside or below the table in the documented dialog. Thickness is a cell value. The **Color** field for Dielectric, mask and silkscreen can affect the separate 3D viewer. [Four-Layer figure](https://docs.kicad.org/9.0/en/pcbnew/pcbnew.pdf) · [Row-marker figure](https://docs.kicad.org/9.0/cy/getting_started_in_kicad/getting_started_in_kicad.pdf) · [3D colour behaviour](https://docs.kicad.org/9.0/en/pcbnew/pcbnew.html)

### Via spans

The two-colour toolbar swatch opens **Select Copper Layer Pair**. Its figure shows two endpoint lists on the left, a right-pointing arrow and a presets table on the right headed **Enabled, Layers, Label**. The project stores these pairs as routing destinations. Pressing **V** while routing places a through Via and continues on the other Layer of the active pair. The Via still crosses all copper Layers when the destination is an inner Layer. Microvias and blind/buried Vias have separate placement commands. The Physical Stackup figure shows no span bars. [Routing and Layer pairs](https://docs.kicad.org/9.0/en/pcbnew/pcbnew.html) · [Layer-pair and Stackup figures](https://docs.kicad.org/9.0/en/pcbnew/pcbnew.pdf)

### Table columns and per-Layer and per-Dielectric data

In display order the columns are **Layer, Id, Type, Material, Thickness, a lock-icon column, Color, Epsilon R, Loss Tan**. The icon-only column is easy to miss when counting the text headers. Copper has a thickness in length units. A Dielectric can specify core or prepreg, material, thickness, colour, Dk and Df. The illustrated page has no copper-weight or material test-frequency column. [Figure with lock icon](https://docs.kicad.org/9.0/es/getting_started_in_kicad/getting_started_in_kicad.pdf) · [Four-Layer material example](https://docs.kicad.org/9.0/en/pcbnew/pcbnew.pdf)

On **Board Editor Layers**, the 9.0 manual lists exactly four copper designations: **signal**, **power plane**, **mixed** and **jumper**. They guide the designer. Tracks and Zones can occupy any copper Layer whichever designation is selected. [KiCad 9 Board Editor Layers](https://docs.kicad.org/9.0/en/pcbnew/pcbnew.html)

### Adding, removing and reordering

The copper-count selector sets an even number of Layers. Front and back copper are required. **Add Dielectric Layer** and **Remove Dielectric Layer** change the insulating entries separately. **Swap Layers** moves Tracks or drawings between Layers. A command that reorders the physical copper rows is not stated in the 9.0 manual. [Board Setup and command reference](https://docs.kicad.org/9.0/en/pcbnew/pcbnew.html) · [Physical Stackup controls](https://docs.kicad.org/9.0/en/pcbnew/pcbnew.pdf)

### Templates and material libraries

**Import Settings from Another Board** can import **Board layers and physical stackup** from a `.kicad_pcb` file. The 9.0 figure shows FR4, and KiCad’s unversioned developer reference describes a predefined substrate list with names, relative permittivities and loss tangents. The complete 9.0 catalogue and a shared user-editable material library were not verified. [Import dialog](https://docs.kicad.org/9.0/en/pcbnew/pcbnew.pdf) · [Substrate-list reference](https://docs.kicad.org/doxygen/classDIELECTRIC__SUBSTRATE__LIST.html)

### Symmetry check

The page enforces an even copper count, reports total thickness and offers Dielectric thickness adjustment. The 9.0 manual does not describe a comparison of materials or thicknesses across the midplane. [Board Setup](https://docs.kicad.org/9.0/en/pcbnew/pcbnew.html) · [Physical Stackup controls](https://docs.kicad.org/9.0/en/pcbnew/pcbnew.pdf)

### Impedance readout

The 9.0 figure shows the **Impedance controlled** checkbox without an ohms result or a solved Track width. **Calculator Tools → TransLine** is a separate utility with microstrip and stripline models. **KiCad 10.0** adds a different workflow under **Board Setup → Design Rules → Tuning Profiles**. A profile can calculate Track geometry from the Stackup, signal and reference Layers, and target impedance. For a differential pair, the user enters width or gap and can calculate the other. The profile can be assigned to a Net Class. [9.0 figure](https://docs.kicad.org/9.0/en/pcbnew/pcbnew.pdf) · [9.0 Calculator Tools](https://docs.kicad.org/9.0/en/pcb_calculator/pcb_calculator.html) · [10.0 tuning profiles](https://docs.kicad.org/10.0/en/pcbnew/pcbnew.html)

## Autodesk Fusion Electronics

### Editor layout

From a 2D PCB document, choose **Design → Layers → Layer Stack Manager**. In Autodesk’s four-Layer example, **Layer Stack** is the selected tab in a strip that also contains design-rule pages. Beneath it, draw three regions from left to right: a wide **Layer Pairs** properties table, a **Via Pairs** list and a **Preview** of the section. Total board thickness and **OK**, **Cancel** and **Apply** sit below. These positions come from the guide’s figure transcription. [Editor help](https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/ENU/Fusion-ECAD/files/ECD-SETTINGS-MULTILAYER-TSK.htm) · [Illustrated guide, p. 5](https://damassets.autodesk.net/content/dam/autodesk/www/pdfs/a-comprehensive-guide-to-signal-integrity-simulation-in-pcb-design--what-you-need-to-know.pdf)

### Cross-section drawing and direct interaction

The preview shows the construction and updates when a Via pair changes. The documented input controls are the material cells and **Add Via** dialog. Autodesk does not state the preview’s thickness scale, whether a band selects its table row, or whether an edge can be dragged to resize or reorder an entry. [Stackup and preview help](https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/ENU/Fusion-ECAD/files/ECD-SETTINGS-MULTILAYER-TSK.htm) · [Material editing](https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/ENU/Fusion-ECAD/files/ECD-EDIT-MAT-PROPERTIES-TSK.htm)

### Via spans

**Add Via** opens **From** and **To** selectors. One outer and one inner Layer define a blind span. Two inner Layers define a buried span. For a microvia, choose adjacent Layers and enable **Micro vias**. **Apply** accepts the definition and allows another entry. The manual explicitly says the allowed span appears in both the illustration and the Via Pairs list. Actual Vias are placed during routing. The captions identify the blind and microvia previews without specifying the exact barrel or endpoint glyphs. [Via-pair instructions and figures](https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/ENU/Fusion-ECAD/files/ECD-SETTINGS-MULTILAYER-TSK.htm)

### Table columns and per-Layer and per-Dielectric data

Autodesk identifies an ID (**#**), material, type and thickness. The signal integrity guide identifies copper thickness and substrate thickness, Dk and Df as Stackup inputs. **Edit material properties** enables overrides. A material cell offers a short picker and a detailed selection dialog. An edited material is marked **User Defined**, with an asterisk in its ID cell. The full column order, an independent copper-weight field, a dielectric test-frequency field and a signal/plane/mixed role selector were not verified. [Material editing](https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/ENU/Fusion-ECAD/files/ECD-EDIT-MAT-PROPERTIES-TSK.htm) · [Signal integrity guide, pp. 5 and 7](https://damassets.autodesk.net/content/dam/autodesk/www/pdfs/a-comprehensive-guide-to-signal-integrity-simulation-in-pcb-design--what-you-need-to-know.pdf)

### Adding, removing and reordering

Choose a count preset or use **Add Layer Pair** and **Remove Layer Pair**. Autodesk’s manual sequence starts at the outer faces and adds inner pairs successively. The copper count stays even. Arbitrary reordering of individual copper Layers is not stated in the documentation. [Layer-pair controls](https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/ENU/Fusion-ECAD/files/ECD-SETTINGS-MULTILAYER-TSK.htm)

### Templates and material libraries

A custom `.estackup` preset saves the construction, Layer properties and Via pairs. **Open Preset File** applies it to another board. A material override belongs to the current Stackup and persists in its saved preset. The supplied library record retains its original values. [Preset workflow](https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/ENU/Fusion-ECAD/files/ECD-SETTINGS-MULTILAYER-TSK.htm) · [Material override scope](https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/ENU/Fusion-ECAD/files/ECD-EDIT-MAT-PROPERTIES-TSK.htm)

### Symmetry check

**Maintain Symmetry** starts enabled for conductor or prepreg additions. Resetting a user material also resets its complementary entry. The reviewed help does not describe a mismatch report for an existing Stackup or a choice of which half to copy. [Symmetry setting](https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/ENU/Fusion-ECAD/files/ECD-SETTINGS-MULTILAYER-TSK.htm) · [Complementary reset](https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/ENU/Fusion-ECAD/files/ECD-EDIT-MAT-PROPERTIES-TSK.htm)

### Impedance readout

In **PCB Simulation → Analyze Signal**, the user selects a routed signal, enters target impedance and tolerance, and inspects results below the input fields or as a coloured overlay on the board. Autodesk identifies this as a Signal Integrity Extension feature closed to new purchases. An inline impedance readout in the Stackup preview is not stated in the documentation. [Impedance analysis](https://help.autodesk.com/cloudhelp/ENU/Fusion-ECAD/files/ECD-IMPEDANCE-ANALYSIS-TSK.htm)
